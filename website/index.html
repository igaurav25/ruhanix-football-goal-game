Ruhanix ⚽ Swipe Soccer

The Ultimate Football Web Portal

📜 Official Game Rules

  • Swipe up on the screen to shoot the ball towards the goal post.
  • Score goals to earn XP and level up your player profile.
  • Avoid the moving defender; if they tackle the ball, your shot is wasted!
  • You get exactly 5 shots per match. Score at least 2 goals to win.

🎯 Shooting Modes

  • Classic Free Kick: The standard mode where you aim and shoot past a single moving defender.
  • Time Attack: Score as many goals as possible within 60 seconds! (Unlocks at Level 5)
  • Pro Penalty: Face off against an advanced AI Goalkeeper in a high-stakes penalty shootout. (Unlocks at Level 10)
